## How it works

Uploads land in self-hosted MinIO under uuid names, with metadata in MongoDB. At midnight a cron job mirrors curated files to S3 with `mc mirror`, deletes them from MinIO and invalidates their Redis keys.

Reads never go straight to storage. FastAPI checks Redis first (55-minute TTL on signed URLs), then MinIO, then S3. Noisy traffic stays on the cheap side; only the nightly batch and cache misses reach S3. One Compose file, one `/health` endpoint that pings all four backends.

## Limits

Pipeline, not product. No auth, no delete endpoint, no pagination, and the frontend assumes the API is on `localhost:8000`. The tiering and invalidation hold up; everything around them would need building.
