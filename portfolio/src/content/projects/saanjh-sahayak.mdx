## The problem

In old-age homes, caretakers collect lab reports but doctors review them in
batches — slow, and easy to miss deterioration. The idea: digitize the handoff
so an AI first pass (parameters, summary, severity, specialist suggestion)
reaches the doctor before the visit, not after.

## What exists (prototype)

An 11-screen Expo app (caretaker flows, doctor flows, PDF viewing) over an
Express API with 18 endpoints. Uploaded PDFs land in GridFS; a two-stage
Gemini pipeline first extracts structured health parameters (few-shot, with a
negative example to reject non-medical uploads) and then generates analysis,
precautions, severity 0–10, and specialist routing, filed under the patient's
unverified reports. A doctor's note promotes a report to verified.

## Honest status

Late prototype, stalled since 2024: the pipeline and review loop are real, but
authentication is still stubbed demo sessions — it needs real auth before it
touches real patients. I'm keeping it here because the two-stage extraction
design is the interesting part, not because it's shippable.
