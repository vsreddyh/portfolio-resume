## How it works

Scrapy spiders (English, Hindi, Telugu) crawl verse pages into a trilingual CSV, compiled into a SQLite database bundled as a 2.8 MB app asset and read through `SQLiteProvider` — zero network calls. Around it: an expo-router drawer shell with home, history, settings, terms and privacy screens. The `verses` table holds 701 rows across chapter, verse, English/Hindi/Telugu definitions and shlokas; the `history` table ships empty.

## Limits

The dataset and offline bundling are done — that was the hard part. Remaining work is the reading loop: wiring the home screen past placeholder content, threading chapter/verse through `getVerse()` (currently `SELECT * FROM verses LIMIT 1`), persisting the language choice, and writing reads to the `history` table.
