## How it works

I abandoned the Expo app. What survived is the habit: a scheduled T3 task fetches a verse from the internet every day at a fixed time and opens a thread on it, with text, translations, and commentary. No accounts, no backend, no app to maintain.

The original build — three Scrapy spiders (English, Hindi, Telugu), a 701-row trilingual SQLite corpus, an expo-router shell — is retired with the repo.

## Scope

One verse a day, inside my existing workflow. Nothing to install, nothing to maintain.
