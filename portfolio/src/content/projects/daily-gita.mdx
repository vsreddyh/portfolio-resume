## The problem

Devotional apps shouldn't need accounts, backends, or connectivity. A daily
verse reader is the perfect offline-first app: the entire corpus fits in a few
megabytes, so ship the database with the app and never phone home.

## What exists (in development)

Scrapy spiders crawl English, Hindi, and Telugu verse pages into a trilingual
CSV, compiled into a 701-row SQLite database bundled as an app asset and read
through `SQLiteProvider` — zero network calls. Around it: drawer + tab
navigation via expo-router, a language picker, reading history, and full
in-app Terms/Privacy text framing the offline design.

## Honest status

Late prototype: dataset, bundling, and navigation shell are done; the
verse-reading loop itself is still being wired (the home screen renders
placeholders). The data pipeline was the hard part and it's finished — the UI
is what's left.
