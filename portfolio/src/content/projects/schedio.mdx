## The problem

Student projects are scattered across GitHub profiles nobody finds, and recruiters
have no good way to discover them through colleges. I wanted one place where
students, colleges, and recruiters meet around real work.

## What I built

Schedio is a full-stack collaboration platform with role-based access control and
profile management for all three sides. To solve the cold-start problem, I scraped
data from over 10,000 colleges into an institutional dataset, so the platform has
real content from day one.

Discovery goes beyond keyword search: tokenized search and filtering, plagiarism
detection, and AI-powered code explanations via the Gemini API. Contributors get
file uploads (code, images, videos), analytics, PDF exports, and contributor
management — the unglamorous tooling that makes sharing and evaluation actually work.

## How it runs

Docker for dev/production parity, live deployment on Vercel. Try it:
**[schedio-coral.vercel.app](https://schedio-coral.vercel.app)**.

## What it taught me

Marketplaces die of emptiness, not bad UI. Seeding supply (the college dataset)
mattered more than any feature, and the evaluation tooling — plagiarism checks,
code explanations — is what makes the platform useful to recruiters rather than
just a prettier project list.
