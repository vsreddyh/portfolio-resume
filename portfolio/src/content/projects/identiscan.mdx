## How it works

The kiosk app takes a roll number and a live photo and POSTs both to Express. Express pulls the enrolled photo from GridFS and forwards the pair to a Flask service, which runs dlib embeddings and returns the match distance at a 0.6 threshold. On a match, Express writes the record.

Check-in before 12:00, checkout after a 6-hour gap, one in/out pair per day — all enforced server-side, so the kiosk can't be edited into marking someone present. The admin app manages batch, class and student with cascade deletes on the records. The kiosk ships as a Linux Electron app for touchscreen hardware.

## Limits

Built and exercised hallway-style for batch check-ins, with batch/class/student management and Linux kiosk packaging. Sessions and bcrypt hashing are in place; the natural hardening from here is route-level guards, photo cleanup on student delete, and a student delete path.
