## How it works

Students type their roll number at a touchscreen kiosk and look at the camera. The system matches the live photo against the enrolled one and marks the check-in or checkout — with the timing rules enforced on the server, so attendance can't be faked from the kiosk itself. An admin console manages batches, classes, and per-student records, and the kiosk ships as a Linux app for touchscreen hardware.

## Scope

Built for supervised, high-volume check-ins: whole batches through one kiosk, no paper, no proxies.
