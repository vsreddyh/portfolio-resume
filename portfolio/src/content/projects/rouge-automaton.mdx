## The problem

Game wikis are huge, slow to search mid-session, and go stale. The idea: a
Discord bot that answers Helldivers 2 intel questions (units, counters,
loadouts, biomes, live war status) from a local cache that refreshes itself —
with a personality players actually enjoy talking to.

## What I built

A Go-only bot (no Python runtime) roleplaying a defected informant. Questions
flow through a think-act loop capped at 3 tool rounds over five model tools —
unit/gear/world search, planet status, wiki search — defined once and shared
between the relay client and an MCP stdio server. Retrieval is Mongo keyword
search with cited context; on a local miss it falls back to live wiki lookup
with source links, plus a live war-status API. A separate watcher polls the
wiki RecentChanges feed every 5 minutes and re-parses only changed pages.

The craft is in the guardrails: allowlist + mention gating with auto-threads,
answers truncated to Discord's 2000-rune limit, the persona compiled into the
binary with serial numbers redacted and every transmission ending in `Over.`,
and failure voiced in character — a 429 gets a "busy spreading democracy"
reply with a liberation ETA, never silence. Four static binaries ship in a
distroless image under Podman Compose (bot, watcher, Mongo), seeded by a
one-shot ingest from checked-in intel data.

## What it taught me

Constraints make bots: capped tool rounds, clamped result counts, and canned
failure modes turn an LLM integration from a demo into something that behaves
in production. And embedding the persona at compile time means the character
survives every redeploy bit-for-bit.
