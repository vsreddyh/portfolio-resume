## About the role

Help build the next generation of AI-powered engineering. Join the AI-native Academy as an early-career software engineer to solve real production challenges, build intelligent automation and strengthen system reliability. With hands-on guidance from a senior mentor, combine software development with production support experience, building the skills to grow into broader product and platform engineering roles.

Investigate support cases, troubleshoot production issues and participate in support rotations, gaining firsthand insight into how complex systems work and where they can improve. The mandate goes beyond resolving individual issues: build software, AI agents and tools that eliminate recurring manual work and prevent problems from returning.

Projects could include agentic Tier 1/2 case automation, automated access workflows, technical knowledge retrieval, log analysis and system hardening. Write, test, review and deploy code, with opportunities to own improvements from identifying the problem to measuring the result. As strong engineering skills and measurable impact are demonstrated, there are opportunities to graduate into core product engineering teams, based on readiness and business needs.

## What you'll do

- Investigate and resolve support cases, production defects, access issues and integration failures.
- Build automation for recurring tasks such as access requests, case responses, log analysis, troubleshooting and maintenance.
- Develop AI agents and workflows that help classify cases, retrieve relevant knowledge and execute approved actions with appropriate safeguards.
- Identify root causes and improve code, monitoring, tests and documentation to prevent recurring incidents.
- Write, test, review and deploy maintainable software using established engineering practices.
- Participate in incident response and scheduled support or on-call rotations, with training and guidance appropriate to your experience.
- Work with senior mentors and product teams to understand systems, prioritize improvements and deliver changes safely.
- Measure the impact of your work through reduced manual effort, fewer repeat issues, faster resolution and improved reliability.

## What you'll bring

- 3+ yrs of programming experience in at least one language, such as Python, Java, JavaScript, TypeScript or Go.
- An understanding of software engineering fundamentals, including data structures, APIs, testing, version control and debugging.
- An ability to investigate problems using logs, error messages and system behavior.
- Curiosity about automation, AI-assisted development and reliable production systems.
- Clear communication, a collaborative approach and a willingness to ask questions and learn from feedback.
- A degree in computer science, engineering or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

## Helpful experience

- Building scripts, internal tools or workflow automation.
- Salesforce platform working knowledge, preferably with Salesforce admin certification and Apex hands-on experience - or desire to obtain certification.
- Working with cloud platforms, databases, CI/CD pipelines or monitoring tools.
- Developing or integrating AI agents, large language models or knowledge retrieval systems.
- Supporting enterprise applications or investigating production incidents.
- Applying secure coding practices, access controls and audit logging.
