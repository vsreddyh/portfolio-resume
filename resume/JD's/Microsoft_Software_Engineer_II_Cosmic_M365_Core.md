## Overview

The Cosmic team in M365 Core IDC builds the hyperscale cloud infrastructure that powers Microsoft 365 services, including M365 Copilot, WorkIQ, IC3, and many other mission-critical experiences. Mission: enable teams across M365 to deliver innovation faster with reliability, safety, and scale.

Core work is COSMIC, Microsoft's managed container platform for cloud services, enabling thousands of M365 services to innovate, deploy, and operate faster with built-in reliability, safety, and operational excellence. Team drives infrastructure investments across deployments, capacity management, networking, storage, security, reliability, and efficiency, including COSMIC Linux Platform, M365 OneBranch Deployment Platform, SDP Infrastructure, and Policy Hub, broadly adopted across Experiences + Devices (E+D).

Next phase: AI-first and agent-driven platform transformation - autonomous and agentic cloud platforms, AI-powered infrastructure for engineering productivity and operational efficiency, deployment velocity, reliability, and safety at hyperscale, distributed systems challenges impacting critical services.
