## What We Do

At Goldman Sachs, we connect people, capital and ideas to help solve problems for our clients. Leading global financial services firm providing investment banking, securities and investment management services to corporations, financial institutions, governments and individuals.

## Who we are / Team

Digital Client Experience teams in Asset Management provide innovative, customer-centered financial products. Three main businesses, underpinned by engineering, product operations, security and risk management:

- **Mosaic:** end-to-end open architecture technology platform and digital product providing trading, settlement, analytics, and reporting solutions to global liquidity clients.
- **GSAM.com:** asset management arm supervising more than $1.8 trillion in assets; discretionary investment advisory services since 1988.
- **Advisor Portal:** external portal, key component of SMA expansion program; all-digital experience for advisors (onboarding, SMA applications, account status tracking, advisor tools). Retail distribution requires scaling onboarding and portfolio management to tens of thousands of clients.

The team builds global web and mobile platforms for Third Party Distribution and Institutional businesses, at the forefront of technology innovation.

## Role

As an engineer on the Digital Client Experience team, drive whole features from design to execution and deep-dive performance (e.g. why an API is half a second slower than it should be). Problems span the entire stack. Help evolve product strategy with creative, commercial solutions and leadership in execution.
