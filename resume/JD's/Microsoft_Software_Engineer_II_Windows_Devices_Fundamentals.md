## Overview

The Windows + Devices Fundamentals team owns reliability, compatibility, performance, battery life, and sustainability of Windows devices. Work spans hardware and OEM partners, the operating system, and application layers, protecting, managing, and enhancing Windows ecosystem fundamentals. Seeking a self-driven Software Engineer II for next-generation Windows device optimizations as AI, silicon, and software advances create new opportunities. Ideal for an engineer who enjoys investigating complex systems and independently delivering innovations affecting how people live, work, and play.

## Responsibilities

- Analyze telemetry and trace data to characterize software and hardware behavior, identify patterns, and assess impact on individual devices and at scale.
- Diagnose system issues using low-level debugging and performance-analysis tools, collaborating with experienced engineers on complex investigations.
- Identify optimization opportunities and own design and implementation of well-scoped improvements.
- Deliver robust, high-quality solutions considering performance, reliability, maintainability, and consistency with broader architecture.
- Use data to evaluate tradeoffs, prioritize work, and make sound decisions in partially defined problem spaces.
- Collaborate with internal teams, OEMs, and hardware partners to deliver improvements, resolve dependencies, and share findings across the Windows ecosystem.

## Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in CS or related technical discipline and 4+ years software engineering experience using C, C++, C#, Java, JavaScript, or Python, or equivalent practical experience.
- 2+ years experience in systems analysis, debugging, performance engineering, or related systems-development work.
- Experience diagnosing software or system issues and contributing to performance or reliability improvements.
- Experience developing, testing, and delivering reliable production software.
- Strong programming, debugging, and problem-solving skills.
- Ability to work independently on well-scoped problems, navigate ambiguity, and use data to guide technical decisions.
