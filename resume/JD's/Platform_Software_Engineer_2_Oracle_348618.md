## Short description

Delivers routine and some non-routine platform work under moderate supervision, including minor enhancements to shared services, SDKs, or integration adapters. Troubleshoots standard issues across components using established playbooks, helping maintain reliability and backward compatibility. Monitors and analyzes platform API/SDK usage for a subsystem; proposes small improvements to observability and resilience. Updates migration notes and reference implementations within defined guidelines.

## Key responsibilities

### Platform Software Development

- Deliver minor enhancements to shared services, integration adapters, or SDK surfaces within defined guardrails.
- Apply compatibility/versioning rules during fixes and minor rollouts to protect downstream teams.
- Propose small observability/resilience improvements based on API/SDK usage signals for a subsystem.

- Contribute to designing software solutions and analyze requirements to achieve business and operational goals, with limited guidance.
